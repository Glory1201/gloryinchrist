<div class="about-card">

I was born into a Muslim family and shaped my earliest understanding of God. My childhood wasn’t easy — I went through abuse that left me questioning how a good God could allow so much pain. As I got older, that hurt turned into rebellion and I made choices I’m not proud of particularly after I lost my mum at 17!

For years, I wrestled with the idea of God. I doubted Him, argued with Him and tried to make sense of everything I’d lived through. But even in my lowest moments, I couldn’t shake the feeling that something can’t come from nothing. Deep down, it was harder to believe God didn’t exist than to believe He did.

It wasn’t until later in adulthood that things started to make sense. Looking back, I began to see purpose woven through my pain. That search to understand Allah is what eventually led me to Jesus — something I never expected. The journey hasn’t been easy, but it’s been real.

This blog is my way of sharing that journey, an honest look at what it feels like to be an ex‑Muslim seeking God. And one day, I hope it will help me share my story with my family.

My hope is that my experiences will encourage anyone who finds themselves where I once stood.


</div>
